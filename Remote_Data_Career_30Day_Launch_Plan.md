**Daily commitment:** 4 hrs learning + 2 hrs profile/applications = 6 hrs/day
**Goal:** First paid gig (freelance or full-time) within 30-45 days

## ⚠️ Reality Check First (Read This Twice)

You're in a great spot, but I won't sugarcoat the math:

- **Junior data scientist roles at US/EU companies** typically require 1-2 years of experience or a finished degree/bootcamp. You'll start applying these aggressively *during* and *after* Moringa, not now.
- **Realistic 30-day target:** Land your first **freelance gig** ($50-$300) on Upwork, Fiverr, or via direct outreach. This is 100% doable.
- **Realistic 60-90 day target:** Land a junior remote contract role ($800-$2,500/month) at a startup, or stack multiple freelance clients.
- **Realistic 6-month target:** Full-time junior remote role at a mid-size US/EU company ($25K-$60K/year, often more with US clients).

**The single biggest mindset shift:** Stop calling yourself an "aspiring data scientist." Start calling yourself a **Data Analyst**. Here's why it matters:

| "Data Scientist" | "Data Analyst" |
|---|---|
| Implies ML, stats wizardry, PhD-adjacent | Implies SQL, Excel, dashboards, business questions |
| ~1% of job postings for entry level | ~30% of job postings for entry level |
| Competitive with PhDs | Competitive with bootcampers |
| Average $130K (US) | Average $75K (US, still excellent) |

You can pivot to "data scientist" once you have Moringa + 1 year of analyst experience. Get hired first.

Based on current 2026 hiring trends, this is your most hireable skill combination for the next 30 days:

1. **SQL (60% of your learning time)** — The single most-requested skill in every data job posting. Easier to learn than Python. Lands gigs faster.

## 💼 LinkedIn Optimization (The Recruiter Magnet)

LinkedIn is where 90% of remote tech recruiters search. Treat it like SEO.

### Your Profile Photo
- Plain background, good lighting, smile, dressed business-casual
- Use [PhotoFeeler](https://www.photofeeler.com) (free) to test which photo scores best

### Your Banner
- Free option: [Canva](https://www.canva.com) → search "LinkedIn banner data"
- Make it relevant: data visualizations, code snippets, or just your name + tagline

### Your Headline (CRITICAL — most-read part of your profile)
**Bad:** "Aspiring Data Scientist | Student"
**Good:** "Data Analyst | SQL, Python, Tableau | Helping startups make data-driven decisions"
**Better:** "Data Analyst specializing in [your niche] | SQL × Python × Tableau | Open to remote opportunities"

Why? Recruiters search by keywords. "Open to remote" is a magnet phrase. Specifying skills makes you discoverable.

### Your About Section (template)
```
I help [your niche, e.g., e-commerce startups] turn messy data into clear business decisions.

🔧 Skills: SQL (advanced), Python (Pandas, NumPy), Tableau, statistical analysis
📊 Recent work: [Link or mention your portfolio project]
🎯 Currently: Building expertise via Moringa School's Data Science program (in partnership with Flatiron School)
🌍 Open to: Remote freelance projects and full-time roles globally

Let's talk if you need someone who can write the SQL, do the analysis, AND explain it to non-technical stakeholders.

📩 [your email]
```

### Skills Section
Add ALL 50 skills. Top 3 should be: SQL, Python, Data Analysis. Then add Tableau, Power BI, Pandas, Microsoft Excel, Data Visualization, Statistical Analysis, Database Management, ETL, Dashboards, Business Intelligence, etc.

### "Open to Work" Setting
Turn it ON for recruiters only (not visible publicly). LinkedIn → Profile → Open to → Finding a new job. Specify "Remote" and your target geographies.

### Daily LinkedIn Habit (15 min/day)
- Post once every 3 days about something you learned (be specific: "Today I learned how SQL window functions can solve X problem...")
- Comment thoughtfully on 5 posts from people in your target industry
- Connect with 5 new people daily (data analysts, engineering managers, founders)

This builds you to ~500 connections in 90 days. The algorithm starts pushing your posts to recruiters once you cross ~300.

Mass-applying converts at ~1%. Cold outreach to specific people converts at ~10%.

- Use [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io) or [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io) (free tiers) to find emails

## 💰 Getting Paid (Critical for Kenyans)

International clients won't send M-Pesa. You need:

### Recommended setup
1. **[Wise](https://wise.com) (formerly TransferWise)** — Get USD, EUR, GBP virtual accounts. Receive payments like a local. Lowest fees. Withdraw to KES. **Open this Day 1.**
2. **[Payoneer](https://www.payoneer.com)** — Required by Upwork and Fiverr. Open it.
3. **[Deel](https://www.deel.com)** — For full-time remote roles. Companies hire you as contractor through Deel. They handle compliance.
4. **PayPal** — Has issues in Kenya for receiving (you can send but receiving has restrictions). Avoid relying on it.

### Tax note
Kenyan freelancers earning from abroad are required to report income to KRA. Consult an accountant once you're earning consistently. Not a concern in your first month, but plan for it.

## ⚠️ Scams & Red Flags to Avoid

These will target you on Upwork, LinkedIn, and Reddit:

- **"Pay $X for a course/certification to get hired"** — 100% scam if unsolicited
- **"Send a sample" for a 4-hour task with no payment** — That's free labor. Decline politely.
- **Telegram/WhatsApp recruiter who can't verify their company** — Almost always fake/MLM
- **"Buy our crypto/data for guaranteed returns"** — Run
- **Clients who want to pay outside the platform** on first job — Upwork bans this and scammers love it
- **"$5,000/month, just submit your bank details"** — Identity theft

If a deal feels too good in week 1, it is.
